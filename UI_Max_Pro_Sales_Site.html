The Subscription Trap

You’re not paying for software. You’re paying rent on your own work.

Every month, per seat, forever. The bill grows when you hire, never shrinks when you ship. And the moment you stop paying, your team loses access to the files they built.

  • Compounding tax. A 5-seat team on Figma Professional pays ~$4,050 over three years — for software that never becomes theirs.
  • Cloud lock-in. Stop paying, lose access. Your design system, your history, your comments — all gone the day the subscription lapses.
  • Tool fragmentation. Design in one tool, prototype in another, hand off in a third. Each adds a subscription and a friction tax.
